`regexp` matching: bound the work of a single PCRE2 match

`regexp` matching, `$(list-search --mode pcre)`, patterndb `@PCRE@` parsers, multi-line `regexp` patterns and `http()` `response-body-action()` patterns: the work PCRE2 may do for a single match is now explicitly bounded (match and depth limit of 1 million, 10 times lower than the PCRE2 default). A pattern that exceeds it (typically nested quantifiers such as `(a+)+$` against attacker influenced content) counts as not matching, as before, and is reported with a single rate limited warning naming the pattern instead of an anonymous error for every message.
